# Login Feature: Product Requirements

## Purpose
Registered customers must be able to sign in to see their orders and saved details.

## Password rules
A password must have at least 8 characters, including one number and one capital letter.

## Account lock
After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. The customer sees a message that tells them how long to wait.

## Session timeout
A signed-in customer is signed out automatically after 30 minutes of no activity.

## Password reset
A customer can ask for a password reset link by email. The link works for 1 hour and can be used only once.
